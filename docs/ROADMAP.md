# Roadmap

Public roadmap. The owner's detailed working plan is kept outside the repository.

## Phase 0: foundation
Astro static site, content schemas and validator, token-based design system with five era themes, Cloudflare Worker serving the site, CI with secret scanning and content validation, contribution guidelines.

## Phase 1: first catalog
Twelve exhibits across six decades covering PLCs and DCS from the United States, Germany, Japan and Brazil, each with sources, lifecycle information and five-language text (pt and en human-verified; es, de, it machine-translated and flagged for native review).

## Phase 2: the stage
Cover Flow stage with keyboard, touch and reduced-motion support; timeline scrubber; Era UI toggle; share cards.

## Phase 3: community stories
Story submission form with Turnstile, moderation by the maintainer, stories published in the contributor's own language with on-demand machine translation.

## Phase 4: launch and weekly cadence
One new or updated exhibit per week, driven by community proposals and stories.

## Later
Educational modules (the same motor start in LD, IL, FBD and ST; PLC versus DCS architectures), lifecycle and migration references for engineers who still run these systems, native curators per language.

Contributions are welcome at every phase: see `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
