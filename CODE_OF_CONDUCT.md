# Code of conduct

PLC Museum is a place where people who spent nights in control rooms meet people who are learning what a rung is. Everyone is welcome: engineers and technicians of any generation, students, historians, former employees of the companies whose products appear here, and anyone curious.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific. Disagree about facts with sources, not about people.
- Credit others' work and memories; never post someone else's story or photo as your own.
- Keep brand rivalries playful. A controller you hated was someone else's daily bread.
- Write for a global audience in whichever of the site's languages you prefer.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing private information (including a contributor's contact details), sexualized content, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to `hello@plcmuseum.com`. The maintainer reviews every report, responds within 7 days, and may remove content, lock threads, or ban accounts, depending on severity. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), the site's story submissions, and project spaces on social networks. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
